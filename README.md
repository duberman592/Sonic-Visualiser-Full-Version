**Sonic Visualiser** is an innovative audio analysis tool designed for professionals and enthusiasts alike, developed by the Centre for Digital Music at the University of London. Whether you're a sound engineer, musicologist, or just curious about the intricacies of sound, **Sonic Visualiser** allows you to perform a comprehensive analysis of audio files with incredible precision.

## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Multi-format Support** — Analyze audio files in WAV, OGG, and MP3 formats.
- **Waveform Visualization** — View and inspect waveforms of songs in multiple formats.
- **Data Input & Analysis** — Input analysis data at any point or segment of a song for detailed insights.
- **Simultaneous Time Resolution** — Analyze data in multiple time resolutions at once for comprehensive studies.
- **Plugin Integration** — Launch various plugins, including beat detection and tone analysis, to enhance your workflow.

### Advanced Features:
- **MIDI File Import** — Import notes from MIDI files and visualize them alongside frequency scales.
- **Loop Playback** — Select specific audio segments and play them in loops seamlessly.
- **Time Stretching** — Adjust the playback speed of songs with total precision.
- **Audio Export** — Easily export selected audio regions for further use.
- **Multiprocess Capability** — Utilize your hardware effectively for optimal performance.
